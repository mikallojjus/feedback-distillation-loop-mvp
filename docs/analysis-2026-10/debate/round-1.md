## 3. A Tuesday with the loop

Tuesday, after lunch. I open Claude Code in the geo-agents folder and type /claim-tagger status. Three lines back: nothing paused, 50 rows and one rule change wait for me, Next: decide the rows. I click the link. Your queue shows four columns: Claim, Tag outcome, Why wrong, Your reasoning. The claim is the row title, so I read it in place. Most rows are a plain Applies or Does not apply. When I disagree with the agent I pick Why wrong and type one sentence, and I name the rule when I mean the rule. Context-dependent rows make me think; some take three minutes. After forty minutes I have done eighteen rows. Thirty-two stay in the queue, which I accept because the report said nothing is lost.

Then Lessons waiting: one row. The view shows the lesson name and status but not the two texts, so I open the row, read Current text against Proposed text, set Approved. Nobody has said whether I type /claim-tagger promote or the engineer does; I type it, the one-line "about 2 minutes" notice appears, and I do other work until the three-part report says PASS.

What annoys me: the mirror's Agent notes column has become a paragraph per claim, which the whole team sees; "6 model calls" in the report tells me nothing; the demo taught me "Decide these rows" and the real page says "Your queue"; and I have twelve minutes of my hour left, into which the "optional 30-minute review" does not fit.
