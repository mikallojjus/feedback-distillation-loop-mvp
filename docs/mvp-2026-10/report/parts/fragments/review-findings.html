Cross-questions

Each persona answered the other's questions (review/persona-engineer-r1-answers.md, review/persona-editor-r1-answers.md). Three answers changed the design. The editor asked what a FAIL card means for them: nothing changes, decisions continue under the written rule, and the lesson stays Approved; because the "Lessons waiting" view then hid it, Rule changes gained a computed Owner action column that keeps a failed or held lesson in front of the rule owner with the next step spelled out (decision M13). The editor asked for rows handed on (Needs evidence, Ask another editor) to be visible on the hub: a hub view now lists them. The editor and the engineer disagreed on whether a decided row should be re-queued after a rule change; the design keeps decided rows as permanent tests and uses the follow-up (Fixed or Still wrong) instead (decision M12).
