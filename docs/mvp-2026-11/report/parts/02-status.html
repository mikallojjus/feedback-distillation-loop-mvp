Status

In short

The MVP is built, tested and run live: the second checkpoint, the final demo, is reached. The loop runs end to end on a copy of {{count:plan.claimsCopy}} real claims in Notion: the agent tags, the editor decides, a rule change is drafted from the decisions, a safety check tests it against every earlier decision before the rulebook changes, and the follow-up re-checks the rows. The whole cycle ran {{count:calibration.pagesAll}} times on practice pages, through to a rule going live and being rolled back again. After the demo was handed over, the product owner asked that editors decide where they work: since 16 October the decision lives on the claims table itself, in the same columns the real table has, and the former queue is the agent's own record. On 22 October the agent also took over the reason behind each decision: editors fill their verified tags, their notes and the status, and the agent reads every disagreement itself, tag by tag, as a slip of its own, an unclear rule or a rule that should change, and drafts from what it read.

The MVP judges one tag, Context-dependent, and never writes the real claims table. Everything else waits in the Backlog.

What the product owner does now. Run the seven steps on the fresh practice page built for the 22 October revision, as the How to run tab describes, and say whether the MVP is agreed and the revision's branch may be merged.
