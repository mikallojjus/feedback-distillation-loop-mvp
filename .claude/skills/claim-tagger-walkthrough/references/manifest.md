# The manifest: what a practice page holds, how to change it, and the calibration

File: `agents/claim-tagger-mvp/walkthrough/manifest.json`. Every practice page is built from it; `walkthrough.mjs start` reads it (or `--manifest <path>`). Current version: 2026-10-16.1 (the page changed shape on 16 October: editors decide on the Claims table, Tag decisions is the agent's ledger; `calibration.status` is `pending` until three fresh pages have been run through the new shape).

## Content

- `tag`: the real Context-dependent row of the taxonomy (Name, Description, When to use with its five rules, Example Claims, Counterpart Self-contained, Category, Polarity, Status Verified, Work tags, Geo Status, Geo Tag ID, Evidence, Verified on, `copied_from`). The practice page's Rulebook has this one row.
- `counterpart`: the Self-contained row, kept for its example claims only (it is not on the page).
- `canaries`: the ten example claims the safety check reads: the six of Context-dependent (expected to apply) and four of the five of Self-contained (expected not to apply). `canaries_note` says why the fifth, the domestic-chips claim, is left out: under the rulebook in force the judge reads it Applies at 0.3 to 0.8, so it would fail a safety check for reasons that have nothing to do with the editor's lesson. The builder adds `"tag": "Context-dependent"` when it writes `evals/canaries.jsonl`.
- `claims`: the seven real claims, in story order, with the thirteen Geo columns from the dry run of the real table (`key`: presspool, aiforce, cnn, dallas, contractor, kennedy, stopgap; `short`: the claim cut to about 60 characters).
- `decided`: the five claims an editor has Reviewed (cnn and dallas have the tag; contractor, kennedy and stopgap do not). Each entry describes the claim as the page creates it, by the columns of the Claims table: `Claim quality tags — verified` (a list of tag names; the builder turns it into the relation to the page's Rulebook row), `Review status` (Reviewed), `Why wrong` ("Agent slip") and `Challenged tags notes` (the editor's reasoning from the audit of 9 October, "From the audit of 9 October: ..."; the one-time seeding exception of hard rule 1, which the plan check allows on a create while the page's `runs/SETUP` marker exists). `Agent proposal` is the agent's pre-audit answer: it goes to the claim's row in Tag decisions, together with the outcome, Why wrong and reasoning copied from the claim (worked out the way the tag step copies a decision; Decided under stays empty). The ledger has a row for each of these five claims and none for the two open ones.
- `open`: the two claims the editor decides at step 3, both on rule 3: presspool (the agent says Applies; the editor: the tag does not apply, so nothing is verified; Rule unclear; a group every reader knows by its standing role is identified) and aiforce (the agent says Can't tell, or Does not apply at low confidence; the editor: Context-dependent verified, Rule unclear, a capitalised phrase that names no known body is still a kind of thing, not which one). Both are created with `Review status` "To review" and the other three editor columns empty. `enter` holds what the editor types, word for word: `Claim quality tags — verified` as a list of tag names (empty for presspool, `["Context-dependent"]` for aiforce), `Review status` "Reviewed", `Why wrong` and `Challenged tags notes`; `walkthrough.mjs decide --claim <key>` writes exactly these four columns. `calibrate` is what the calibration expects from the agent at step 2.
- `alternates`: claims that can replace an open row if the calibration fails on it (two Fairford withdrawal claims and the USPS ballot portal claim, judged Applies at 0.80 to 0.93), and the retired rows with the reason each left the story: Georgia and Melania (decision 33), COVID-19 (decision 37).
- `views`: Your queue in step 3 (a view of the Claims table), Lessons waiting in step 5. `expect`: the counts a fresh page has (`rulebook_rows`, `claims`, `ledger_rows`, `reviewed_claims`, `open_claims`, `linked_views`, `tab_views`), the three views of the Claims table (`claims_views`) and the rule the lesson is on. `steps`: the seven steps with their commands and the tables they affect. `quiet_columns`: Agent settings' Last run summary, rewritten by every command and not required in the step tables.
- `calibration`: `status` (`pending` or `frozen`) and `runs` (one entry per calibration run: date, manifest version, demo id and page, the agent's answer per open row, how the earlier decisions and the example claims came out, the lesson, the verdicts of every safety check, the outcome).

## Changing it

Change a claim only by replacing the whole entry (the Geo columns come from the dry run of the real table, `runs/dry-run/<id>/rows.jsonl`, so Geo Sources keeps its links). Keep the story: two open claims on the same rule, one the agent decided against the editor and one it could not decide; five decided claims that cover both outcomes; the example claims the judge gets right under the rulebook in force. Change the page text in `page.md` to match: the shortened claims appear in every step table, the editor's sentence in step 3 must be the manifest's `enter` text for `Challenged tags notes` word for word (a test compares them), and the rule number appears in the callout, the step 4 heading and steps 3 to 7. Bump `manifest_version`, set `calibration.status` back to `pending`, and record why a row left in `alternates`.

## Calibration (before a manifest version ships)

Three fresh practice pages run end to end under the Demos sub-page, entering the manifest's decisions at steps 3 and 5 through `walkthrough.mjs decide` (the four editor columns of a claim) and `approve`. The manifest is frozen when all of these hold: the press pool row gets Applies at a probability of 0.75 or more, citing rule 3, in at least two of the three tag runs; the AI Force row gets Can't tell or Applies at 0.70 or less in at least two; every earlier decision and every example claim comes out as expected in every run; distill drafts exactly one rule change, on Context-dependent rule 3, from the two rows in every run; at least two of the three safety checks PASS on the draft itself, and any FAIL proposes a whole Editor wording text that quotes the claim it names; after step 2 no Agent notes cell has more than two lines, the press pool claim has the tag in Agent tags and nothing waiting (the agent is fairly sure of it, so it is not in Your queue), and the AI Force claim has empty Agent tags and one tag waiting for an editor. Otherwise swap the failing row for an alternate (the row that leaves becomes an earlier decision or an alternate with a note) and repeat. Each run is recorded in `calibration.runs`.

What the earlier versions showed (runs 1 to 4): a row the judge reads both ways between runs (COVID-19, Applies at 0.80 or Does not apply at 0.25) breaks the story in the run where the disagreement vanishes; a lesson placed on a rule the agent did not cite (rule 4, while the press pool row cites rule 3) never flipped the row on the first safety check, and passed only after the editor's wording; an example claim the judge cannot settle under the rulebook in force (the domestic-chips claim) fails a check that has nothing to do with the lesson.
