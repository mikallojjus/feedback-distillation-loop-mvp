# The manifest: what a practice page holds, how to change it, and the calibration

File: `agents/claim-tagger-mvp/walkthrough/manifest.json`. Every practice page is built from it; `walkthrough.mjs start` reads it (or `--manifest <path>`). Current version: 2026-10-15.3.

## Content

- `tag`: the real Context-dependent row of the taxonomy (Name, Description, When to use with its five rules, Example Claims, Counterpart Self-contained, Category, Polarity, Status Verified, Work tags, Geo Status, Geo Tag ID, Evidence, Verified on, `copied_from`). The practice page's Rulebook has this one row.
- `counterpart`: the Self-contained row, kept for its example claims only (it is not on the page).
- `canaries`: the ten example claims the safety check reads: the six of Context-dependent (expected to apply) and four of the five of Self-contained (expected not to apply). `canaries_note` says why the fifth, the domestic-chips claim, is left out: under the rulebook in force the judge reads it Applies at 0.3 to 0.8, so it would fail a safety check for reasons that have nothing to do with the editor's lesson. The builder adds `"tag": "Context-dependent"` when it writes `evals/canaries.jsonl`.
- `claims`: the seven real claims, in story order, with the thirteen Geo columns from the dry run of the real table (`key`: presspool, aiforce, cnn, dallas, contractor, kennedy, stopgap; `short`: the claim cut to about 60 characters).
- `decided`: the five earlier decisions (cnn and dallas Applies; contractor, kennedy and stopgap Does not apply) with the agent's pre-audit answer as Agent proposal, Why wrong "Agent slip" and the editor's reasoning from the audit of 9 October ("From the audit of 9 October: ...").
- `open`: the two rows the editor decides at step 3, both on rule 3: presspool (the agent says Applies; the editor: Does not apply, Rule unclear, a group every reader knows by its standing role is identified) and aiforce (the agent says Can't tell, or Does not apply at low confidence; the editor: Applies, Rule unclear, a capitalised phrase that names no known body is still a kind of thing, not which one). `enter` holds the values the page tells the editor to type, word for word; `calibrate` what the calibration expects from the agent at step 2.
- `alternates`: claims that can replace an open row if the calibration fails on it (two Fairford withdrawal claims and the USPS ballot portal claim, judged Applies at 0.80 to 0.93), and the retired rows with the reason each left the story: Georgia and Melania (decision 33), COVID-19 (decision 37).
- `views`: Your queue in step 3, Lessons waiting in step 5. `expect`: the counts `verify` checks and the rule the lesson is on. `steps`: the seven steps with their commands and the tables they affect. `quiet_columns`: Agent settings' Last run summary, rewritten by every command and not required in the step tables.
- `calibration`: `status` (`pending` or `frozen`) and `runs` (one entry per calibration run: date, manifest version, demo id and page, the agent's answer per open row, how the earlier decisions and the example claims came out, the lesson, the verdicts of every safety check, the outcome).

## Changing it

Change a claim only by replacing the whole entry (the Geo columns come from the dry run of the real table, `runs/dry-run/<id>/rows.jsonl`, so Geo Sources keeps its links). Keep the story: two open rows on the same rule, one the agent decided against the editor and one it could not decide; five decided rows that cover both outcomes; the example claims the judge gets right under the rulebook in force. Change the page text in `page.md` to match: the shortened claims appear in every step table, the editor's reasoning in step 3 must be the manifest's `enter` text word for word, and the rule number appears in the callout, the step 4 heading and steps 3 to 7. Bump `manifest_version`, set `calibration.status` back to `pending`, and record why a row left in `alternates`.

## Calibration (before a manifest version ships)

Three fresh practice pages run end to end under the Demos sub-page, entering the manifest's decisions at steps 3 and 5 through `walkthrough.mjs decide` and `approve`. The manifest is frozen when all of these hold: the press pool row gets Applies at a probability of 0.75 or more, citing rule 3, in at least two of the three tag runs; the AI Force row gets Can't tell or Applies at 0.70 or less in at least two; every earlier decision and every example claim comes out as expected in every run; distill drafts exactly one rule change, on Context-dependent rule 3, from the two rows in every run; at least two of the three safety checks PASS on the draft itself, and any FAIL proposes a whole Editor wording text that quotes the claim it names; after step 2 no Agent notes cell has more than two lines and the two open rows have empty Agent tags and one tag waiting for an editor. Otherwise swap the failing row for an alternate (the row that leaves becomes an earlier decision or an alternate with a note) and repeat. Each run is recorded in `calibration.runs`.

What the earlier versions showed (runs 1 to 4): a row the judge reads both ways between runs (COVID-19, Applies at 0.80 or Does not apply at 0.25) breaks the story in the run where the disagreement vanishes; a lesson placed on a rule the agent did not cite (rule 4, while the press pool row cites rule 3) never flipped the row on the first safety check, and passed only after the editor's wording; an example claim the judge cannot settle under the rulebook in force (the domestic-chips claim) fails a check that has nothing to do with the lesson.
